PC keys for BK keys: Help (?).

On-screen keyboard

Keyboard button at bottom right of screen: BK (BK-0010-01 keyboard), XT (PC keys), JOY (joystick).

Joystick

NumPad 8 4 6 2 directions, 7 9 1 3 5 buttons 1–5. On-screen JOY: PORT off — cursor keys and space (every program, one key at a time), PORT on — port 177714, directions and fire together (programs with joystick support).

Devices

iPhone / iPad

On: music of other apps keeps playing with emulator, ring / silent switch mutes emulator. Off: emulator plays even in silent mode, other apps' sound stops when it starts. iOS 17 or later.

Browsers start sound only after first click or key on page («Tap for sound»).

Windows move by title bar; double click puts window back in place. Corner grip (bottom right) changes size; double click on it: default size.

Window size (Options → Video): chosen size is upper limit, smaller window fits picture. BK picture (512 × 256) is shown 4:3, as on TV.

Keys go to BK while its window is active (lighter title bar): click on screen activates it; buttons above screen keep active window; active File Commander takes keys itself.
